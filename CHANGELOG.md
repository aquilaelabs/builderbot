## Unreleased

### Added

- A theme and a page on the Design view carry rules of their own, for what holds for that theme or that page alone; a session meets one named under its holder (nostromo/hull), bb design show THEME prints a theme with its rules, and the file and the prompts carry them under the theme or the page
- A project's briefing says when its notebook holds a design, what is in it and to read it before building, and on a tree with no code yet that the design, the rules and the roadmap are the specification, so a project designed before its first line is built to the design
- Conversation logs on the Profile tab: your own conversations with Claude, newest first, each downloadable as a text of what was said on both sides, every command Claude ran and what it answered
- Reboot and Shut down on a node's row, under Advanced: the agent schedules the machine's own reboot or power-off a few seconds out so the job reports first. The press names the sessions it ends; dead with the reason while the agent is away; an administrator's only (0d3a6f0)
- Set up GPU display on a node's row (Health): the NVIDIA driver and a headless X server on the card, as bb-sandbox gpu and a unit of the machine's own; after the one reboot it asks for, an emulator there renders on the card and bb emu gpu says so, and a session sees the display (6afdb2e)
- A Claude session can send its project to Docker the way the Artifacts tab's Send to Docker manager does: bb devapp new [NAME] [--branch REF] makes a container from the project's compose file, builds and starts it, and waits for the result, under the project's Claude may: Run its container setting (f02a9a8)
- bb capture: shot, one picture at rest (shots a quarter second apart until a second of them match), and reel, the frames that changed on one sheet with a JSON of when; each from a page, the running emulator or an X display. bb emu shot is bb capture shot --emu (60d809c, 0a56328)
- The notebook has a Design view: a project's design language (colors as tokens, type, shape, space, motion, your rules) and pages laid out as labeled blocks on a phone or desktop frame; bb design and docs/design-language.md carry it, and nothing on the view reaches into a session (8d6375a)
- A project's settings name other projects a session on it may read: their code read-only in its view on a node, their notebooks through bb from inside them, every write refused by name. That list, and the nodes a project also works on, are checkbox listboxes (206c6d6)
- The Design view holds themes: each a name, dark or light, and its own colors, added and cut one by one with what each is for; a preset fills the ten, the app starts on the first, and a theme picker asked of a session takes them from here (2a6b656, 74e517a)
- A session's save of a project's design goes through its own dial, Claude may: Add to the design, auto by default; a design's notes are named rules with triggers a session is handed one at a time, like house rules; and a session reaches the containers of the projects its own reads, for bb capture (d8338f5)

- A custom choice of a design's language can name the rule that says it, and that rule's text is what a session reads in the choice's place (6bb5358)
- A page on the Design view can have a short key of its own, which its rules are addressed under (now/live) in place of the whole of its name (6bb5358)
- bb toolchains ends with a builds-with block: the platforms and build-tools in the store, the compileSdk ceiling and what a library past it fails on, and what the Gradle cache holds (wrapper distributions, AGP, Kotlin, the compose BOM), so a session does not list them by hand (85ca0dd)
- bb emu touch tap|hold|glide sends a touch with its timing kept through sendevent, in screen pixels and turned with the screen, and a capture's --drive script gets it as $BB_TOUCH: adb's own input verbs cost 300 ms a call and cannot time a glide or tell a long-press from a slide (85ca0dd)
- A test run in progress is said on the dashboard where the app already reads it: the project's row says tests running and since when, and the notebook's Tests view names the run, who started it and the command before the last run's numbers (85ca0dd)

- A project's settings keep its Android signing keys, a Play upload key and a sideload signing key: checked with keytool before they are kept, held by the controller where no session can read them, with a backup download and a warning until a sideload key has one (fcb44bf)
- A build in dist/ is signed with a project's kept key as a job on its node: the key reaches only that job, in a directory sessions cannot read, and the signed build arrives as NAME-signed with its certificate checked (4e7eb3e)
- Every project has an owner, who can hand it on and make it invite only, so administrators cannot add themselves (BuilderBot's owner always can); people are invited from the project's settings and accept or decline from their bell (b8d747c)
- A project's owner offers it to someone else, who becomes its owner on accepting from their bell; BuilderBot's owner still hands a project on at once (83d43e6)
- bb sign signs a build in dist/ with one of the project's keys on its node, behind the project's dial Claude may: Sign a release (ask by default, which waits for the project's owner in their bell); the Artifacts tab offers Sign on an .apk or .aab (00129fe)
- A signed build's Artifacts row says which key signed it, what verified, and the certificate's whole fingerprint, with Copy for F-Droid for its AllowedAPKSigningKeys (01af12e)
- A project keeps several named signing keys, uploaded or generated on the controller, and maps each app's package to one, so a repository of several apps signs each with its own key or a shared one; Claude may propose a key or a mapping under Manage signing keys (d32bb3d)

- A project's owner can put a password on its signing keys' backups, which every download then asks for, BuilderBot's owner's included; five wrong in a row hold downloads for fifteen minutes (81c9831)

### Security

- A terminal on a node now opens only for the account the controller vouches for, so a session can no longer reach the node's terminal server directly and open one as another account or on another project (B166) (77d2631)
